A LITTLE RESET / 好好收拾 — 0.6.0

Original Season One workbenches and corner-shop diorama geometry, interface,
procedural wood, enamel, grime and dial textures, animations and synthesized
audio and the Slow Hours arpeggio created for this game. RESTO is the
fictional coffee appliance in this scene.

Three.js 0.185.1 — MIT License, Copyright Three.js authors.
Includes RoundedBoxGeometry and RoomEnvironment from the Three.js examples.
See licenses/THREE-MIT.txt.

Rapier JavaScript compatibility package 0.12.0 — Apache License 2.0.
Physics engine by Dimforge and contributors, https://rapier.rs/.
See licenses/RAPIER-APACHE-2.0.txt.

Cleaning uses raycast surface coordinates and a persistent grime mask.
Loose accessories use rigid-body physics; their final placement is assisted
with a short snap animation. Water, lighting and record playback are stylized
procedural effects rather than a general real-world simulation.

Default background music: 光落在窗台 / After the Light, an original piano
composition created for Quiet Workshop and rendered from its original score.

Piano samples: Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm (Yamaha C5).
Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported.
https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
Samples were resampled, transposed, velocity-layer mixed and processed with
pedal envelopes, room ambience and mastering. No endorsement is implied.
Full sample license: licenses/SALAMANDER-CC-BY-3.0.txt.

Season Three: original lighthouse post-office scenes, models, labels and
fictional letter/parcel interactions created for this game. No real messages
or parcels are sent by the game.

Season four Mountain Station: original code-built miniature railway, station objects, mountain scenery and card illustrations. The train journey and timetable are fictional game scenes.
