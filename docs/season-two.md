# 第二季 · 雨后花房

Status: 0.4.0 published; 120 tests passed, local browser verification and all 9 public file hashes verified. Player acceptance and real-device performance remain separate. See `../qa/season-two-v0.4.0/REPORT.md`. Baseline: first season 0.3.1, migrated to a standalone project without changing save keys or published files.

## Direction

Nine restorations in one greenhouse after rain. Chapter one: 花盆、工具、育苗. Chapter two: 花架、玻璃、流水. Chapter three: 灯笼、长椅、花房苏醒. Preserve cleaning, placement and quiet completion, while adding wider 3D inspection and visible assembly results. Each completed level restores part of the final greenhouse.

## Acceptance

- Both seasons remain selectable; their progress and permanent collection are independent.
- Every level can progress through cleaning, placement, finishing and completion, including resume after reload.
- Phone controls retain one-finger cleaning; rotating viewpoints is explicit and does not scrub accidentally.
- Scene material, light and final operation give a visible before/after change.
- Desktop and phone viewport evidence, save tests and public build verification are recorded separately.
